# Company Data Retention Policy {#data-retention}

## Purpose {#purpose}

This policy defines how company data must be retained and disposed of.

## Scope {#scope}

Applies to all employees and contractors who handle company data.

## Retention Periods {#retention-periods}

Financial records must be retained for seven years.
HR records must be retained for five years.

### Exceptions {#exceptions}

Legal holds may extend standard retention periods indefinitely.
